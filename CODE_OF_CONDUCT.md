# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in JSON Render Extended a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback with empathy.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing credentials or personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/json-render-extended/json-render-extended/security/advisories/new)
and mark the report as a community conduct concern. Maintainers may remove or reject contributions,
comments, issues, or participation that violate this policy. Enforcement decisions should be fair,
proportionate, and focused on protecting the community.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
